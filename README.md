Ethiopian Biology & Healthcare Opportunities Website
A web development project designed to help Ethiopian high-school students discover extracurricular opportunities in biology, healthcare, and related fields.
Project Overview
Finding relevant extracurricular activities can be challenging for high-school students who want to explore their academic interests, gain practical experience, and strengthen their future university applications.
This project was developed to address that challenge by creating a centralized website where Ethiopian students can explore opportunities related to biological sciences and healthcare.
The website was inspired by a project proposal focused on improving access to educational and extracurricular resources for students, particularly those who face geographical or informational barriers.
Problem Statement
Information about internships, volunteer programs, research opportunities, and other extracurricular activities is often scattered across different platforms and sources. Students interested in biology and healthcare may struggle to find opportunities that align with their interests and goals.
The goal of this project is to make relevant opportunities easier to discover through a dedicated, accessible web platform.
Project Objectives
·	Make biology- and healthcare-related extracurricular opportunities easier for Ethiopian high-school students to discover.
·	Provide a centralized place to explore relevant opportunities and resources.
·	Encourage students to pursue their academic interests beyond the classroom.
·	Support students interested in future studies and careers in biological sciences and healthcare.

·	CSS — styling and page layout.

Target Audience
The primary target audience is Ethiopian high-school students interested in biology, healthcare, and related academic or career pathways.
The project also aims to address the needs of students who may have difficulty accessing information about relevant opportunities because of geographical or informational barriers.
Development Process
I developed this website independently using HTML and CSS. The project was guided by an initial proposal that identified challenges Ethiopian high-school students face when searching for relevant extracurricular opportunities.
The development process involved translating the project's purpose into a website prototype and organizing its content into dedicated pages.
